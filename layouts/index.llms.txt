# Rakovac (rakovac.rs)

> Unofficial visitor portal of Rakovac (Serbian: Раковац), a village on the northern slope of Fruška Gora in
> Vojvodina, Serbia, 13 km from Novi Sad, in Beočin municipality. Nature and walks, Rakovac Monastery, 7,000 years
> of history (the Gradina / Dombó archaeological site), people, places to stay, local food and wine, bus timetable.
> Made by Konstantin (Kosta) Stein, who lives in Rakovac. Available in Serbian (Latin), English, Russian, German and
> Hungarian. Facts are checked against sources; unconfirmed details are marked on the pages.
