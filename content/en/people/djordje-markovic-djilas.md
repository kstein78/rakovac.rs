Commander of the staff of the partisan detachments for Srem. He was killed together with Stanko Paunović Veljko and the courier Zencel Hunjadi at Stručica in Rakovac during the breakout of August 1942. His name is on the middle pillar of the [Stručica memorial]({{< relref "history/strucica-memorial" >}}).
