The hermitage (*isposnica*) in the forest east of Beli Majdan is named after him: his name is carved in the oldest inscription beside the entrance. Published sources date this inscription to 1711 or 1751. Little else is known about him. See [Hermitage of Isaija Parivodski]({{< relref "nature/isposnica" >}}).
