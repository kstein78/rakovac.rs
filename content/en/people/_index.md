This glossary grows over time. Historical entries are based on published sources. For people living today, we record conversations on camera, with their permission: memories of the village, the monastery, the war years, the quarry and everyday life. Where a video exists, you will find it on the person's page.

Would you like to tell your story, or do you know someone who should? See [About the author]({{< relref "author" >}}).
