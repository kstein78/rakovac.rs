After a great fire, around 1692–93, Abbot Teofan obtained a protective decree (*ferman*) from the Sultan and began to rebuild the monastery. He travelled to Russia and brought back money, liturgical books and church objects. He edited the **Rakovac Srbljak**, the collection of hymns to Serbian ruler-saints completed in 1714 by the monk Maksim.
