A courier of the staff of the Srem partisan detachments, known as **"Franja"**. He was killed at Stručica in August 1942. His first name appears as both Zencel and Vencel in different sources; the plaque at the memorial reads ЗЕНЦЕЛ ХУЊАДИ.
