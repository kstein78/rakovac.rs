So many church leaders took their monastic vows at Rakovac, or lived here at some point, that it became known as the **"court monastery"** of the Karlovci Metropolitanate. Sources name more than twenty bishops, among them:

- Pavle Nenadović
- Mojsije Putnik
- Danilo Jakšić
- Josif Šakabenta
- Pantelejmon Živković
- Georgije Hranislav
- Sinesije Živanović

Portraits of some of them hang in the monastery's reception rooms.
