Fig opened in the summer of 2023 inside the **Salaxia & Imperator** winery complex, a short walk from Rakovac Monastery. The kitchen is led by chef **Marko Aćimović**, who trained at the ALMA culinary school in Italy. The menu changes with the seasons and is built on domestic produce, preferably from Srem: local cheeses, river fish and vegetables, cooked with Italian technique. The wines come from the winery next door.

A meal here combines well with a morning walk to Beli Majdan or a visit to the monastery.
