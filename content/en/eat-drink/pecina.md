A grill on the road that climbs from the monastery towards Zmajevac, close to the Kamenolom memorial: a red building with stone walls under the wooded hillside, with a fenced terrace by the road. The sign in the window offers grilled meat to eat in or take away, cooked dishes and coffee to go. It is a natural stop before or after a walk: one of the published routes to the Dumbovac waterfall starts right here.

{{< note title="Opening hours" >}}The notice on the door gives 16:00–22:00 on working days and 17:00–22:00 on Sundays. Saturday is not listed separately, so we show it as a working day. In practice Pećina is not always open; when it is, people sit on the terrace over a beer. Call before making a special trip: +381 64 1120206 or +381 21 6275572 (the numbers on the sign in the window).{{< /note >}}
