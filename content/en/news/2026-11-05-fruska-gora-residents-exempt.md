**Update, 9 November:** officially no registration is needed, but nothing is sent by post either: check your plate on the portal yourself. [What is known about charges]({{< relref "news/2026-11-09-fruska-gora-fee-registration-charges" >}}).

Electronic recording and charging in Fruška Gora National Park started on Thursday, 5 November 2026. The payment portal [enaplatafruskagora.co.rs](https://enaplatafruskagora.co.rs/) is now working.

## Who does not pay

People whose permanent address (*prebivalište*) is in one of these **20 municipalities** do not pay for their car: Irig, Beočin, Sremski Karlovci, Novi Sad, Inđija, Ruma, Sremska Mitrovica, Šid, Bačka Palanka, Bač, Bački Petrovac, Bogatić, Odžaci, Stara Pazova, Šabac, Temerin, Titel, Vrbas, Zrenjanin, Žabalj. That includes **Rakovac**, which belongs to Beočin. Some Serbian media write "21 municipalities": in the [official table](https://enaplatafruskagora.co.rs/Home/MunicipalityExemptionInfo) Ruma is listed twice.

No separate registration is needed. The cameras record their cars too, only for traffic and visitor statistics ([021.rs](https://www.021.rs/novi-sad/vesti/460375/pocinje-elektronsko-evidentiranje-i-naplata-za-koriscenje-motornih-vozila-na-fruskoj-gori-ovo-su-detalji), [Ozon](https://www.ozon.rs/vesti/2026/kamere-od-danas-snimaju-vozila-na-fruskoj-gori-ko-placa-a-ko-prolazi-besplatno/)). The exemption comes from the amended article 109 of the law on fees for public goods ([Službeni glasnik RS 35/2026](https://www.parlament.gov.rs/upload/archive/files/cir/pdf/zakoni/14_saziv/153-26.pdf), in force since 6 May 2026).

Companies are not exempt: owners of company vehicles receive a notice with the places, the number of passes and a deadline, after which collection starts.

Other exemptions under the same article: people with disabilities, pre-school children, pensioners with the lowest pension, people working in the park and others. If you qualify but the system does not recognise you (for example your car has plates from another region or another country), apply through the [exemption form](https://enaplatafruskagora.co.rs/Home/ExemptionRequest) with a scan of your ID card and of the vehicle registration (*saobraćajna dozvola*); you can follow the request on the [status page](https://enaplatafruskagora.co.rs/Home/ExemptionRequestStatus).

How the exemption works for foreigners with temporary residence (*privremeni boravak*) whose car has local plates has not been explained officially: the system tells cars apart by their plates, while the law speaks of *prebivalište*. If you receive a charge, apply for the exemption or ask the park.

## How much the others pay

From the park's [fee decision](https://enaplatafruskagora.co.rs/Content/documents/fruskagora/Odluka-o-naknadama.pdf) (last amended on 30 December 2025):

| | 1 hour | Day | Year |
|---|---|---|---|
| I: car, motorcycle | 100 | 300 | 1,800 |
| II: van, with trailer | 150 | 500 | 3,300 |
| III: bus, truck | 200 | 800 | 6,500 |
| IV: over 3 axles | 300 | 2,000 | 18,000 |
| On foot: adult / child | | 100/50 | 600 |

Dinars. "1 hour" is a pass through the park of up to one hour. Category I is up to 1.3 m high; III is higher than 1.3 m.

The cheapest option for driving across the mountain, for example from Rakovac to Ruma, is the one-hour pass for 100 dinars.

## How to pay

Enter your number plate (without spaces or dashes) on [enaplatafruskagora.co.rs](https://enaplatafruskagora.co.rs/). "Provera zaduženja" shows recorded passes with time, place, photo and amount; "Plati unapred" pays before the trip. You can pay by card or with a payment slip at a bank, the post office or by mobile banking ([user guide](https://enaplatafruskagora.co.rs/Content/documents/fruskagora/Korisnicko-uputstvo.pdf)). The [Information Centre on Iriški Venac]({{< relref "nearby/fruska-gora-information-centre" >}}) is closed for reconstruction; advice in person at the park management in Sremska Kamenica, Zmajev trg 1 ([on the map](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=45.2232647,19.8422655)), phone [021 463 666](tel:+38121463666).
