The national park has charged a fee for visits and for driving inside it since 2009. Until now the park's nature wardens collected it on the road, mostly at weekends; neighbours say they took the fee for the car and did not charge the passengers separately. From **5 November 2026** vehicles in the park are recorded electronically ([021.rs](https://www.021.rs/novi-sad/vesti/459992/pocinje-elektronsko-evidentiranje-i-naplata-za-koriscenje-motornih-vozila-na-fruskoj-gori)).

## How much

From the park's [2026 price list](https://fruskagoranovisajt.rs/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/cenovnik.pdf), per day:

- adult on foot: **100 dinars**, child: **50**
- car or motorcycle: **300 dinars**
- van or vehicle with a trailer: 500; bus or truck: 800

[Annual cards](https://fruskagoranovisajt.rs/godisnje-karte/): visitor **600 dinars**, car or motorcycle **1,800**. They are valid for the calendar year, to 31 December.

A one-hour ticket for a car, **100 dinars**, has been reported for the electronic system. It is not in the official price list yet; we will confirm it when the payment portal opens.

## Where to pay

- online: [enaplatafruskagora.co.rs](https://enaplatafruskagora.co.rs/) (on 1 November it still said "portal in preparation") and the [ticket page](https://app.creativedimension.us/gora/korisnickaapp/karte_kupovina.php) linked from the [park's website](https://npfruskagora.co.rs/);
- in person: **not possible for now**, the Information Centre on Iriški Venac is closed for reconstruction ([why and what instead]({{< relref "nearby/fruska-gora-information-centre" >}}), see also the update below);
- annual cards also at the park's offices in Beočin, Vrdnik, Ležimir and Erdevik.

## Who does not pay

The [law](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon-o-naknadama-za-koriscenje-javnih-dobara.html) (article 109) exempts, among others, pre-school children, people with disabilities, and pensioners with the lowest pension (for the car, with the last pension slip). It also lists exemptions for people who live in the park's municipalities and for those who work in the park.

## Local residents and driving through

How the new system will treat local residents' cars, and whether you pay just for driving across Fruška Gora (for example Rakovac – Ruma), is not clear yet. The park has not published rules on this, nor how vehicles are recorded and what the fine is for not paying. Before you rely on anything, ask directly:

- **Fruška Gora National Park:** [021 463 666](tel:+38121463666), [contact form](https://npfruskagora.co.rs/kontakt/)
- **Information Centre on Iriški Venac:** [on the map](https://maps.app.goo.gl/MbSNVzZF94mnHa8y8), in person
- **The park's office in Beočin** (RJ Beočin), which also sells annual cards

We will add the park's answer here as soon as we have it.

**Update, 2 November:** the [Information Centre on Iriški Venac]({{< relref "nearby/fruska-gora-information-centre" >}}) is closed for reconstruction. By phone the park said that for now the fee can only be paid online, and that electronic payment will start in a few days, with details of the exemptions. A park employee said a grace period until the end of the year is likely; this is not official. For advice in person: the park management in Sremska Kamenica, Zmajev trg 1.
