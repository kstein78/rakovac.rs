The public utility **JKP Beočin** announced on its Facebook page on 24 November that the water supply may be interrupted on **Thursday, 26 November 2026, from 8:00 to 12:00** in the villages **from Rakovac to Čerević**. The reason is an announced power cut: without electricity the pumps cannot keep the water flowing. No streets are listed, so the whole of Rakovac should count on it.

## What to do

- On Wednesday evening, fill bottles, a pot or a bucket with enough water for drinking, cooking and washing up until midday.
- After the water comes back it may run cloudy for a short while: let it run before drinking.

The planned power cuts list of Elektrodistribucija for 26 November had no rows for Beočin municipality yet when we checked on the morning of 24 November. We will add the times and streets here if they appear.
