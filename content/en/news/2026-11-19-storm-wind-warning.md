**Update, 19 November, 16:00:** according to RHMZ meteorologist Slobodan Sovilj, the wind will keep strengthening during the day, with the strongest gusts expected **between 21:00 and midnight**. Overnight into Friday the rain stops, the sky clears and the wind weakens ([Danas](https://www.danas.rs/vesti/drustvo/meteorolog-slobodan-sovilj-rhmz-vetar-oluja/)).

On Thursday, 19 November, at 9:10 the RHMZ issued an urgent weather warning and sent emergency SMS messages to people in the affected areas. **This evening**, as a cold front passes, **storm and hurricane-force gusts of westerly and north-westerly wind of 80 to 110 km/h** are expected in Vojvodina, Belgrade and western and central Serbia.

The **red** alert ("particularly dangerous weather that can cause great material damage") covers Belgrade, **Srem**, Banat and **Bačka**; Pomoravlje, western Serbia and Šumadija are on orange. Rakovac lies in Srem, on the slopes of Fruška Gora, where gusts are usually stronger and old trees come down along the forest roads.

The RHMZ forecast from 18 November also says that the weather turns warm again on Friday (16–20 °C), and that from Saturday temperatures will gradually fall, with occasional rain.

## Before evening: house and yard

- **Bring in or tie down everything the wind can pick up**: garden furniture, flower pots, trampolines, umbrellas, bins, tarpaulins, sheets of tin, ladders, boards. Close balcony and greenhouse doors.
- **Close windows and shutters**, fix gates and doors of sheds and stables. Pull the curtains: they help if a pane breaks.
- **Move the car** away from trees, power lines, roofs and scaffolding, preferably into a garage or an open space.
- **Clear gutters and drains** if you can: the front may bring heavy showers.
- **Do not burn leaves or rubbish in the yard**, and check that the stove and chimney are in order: in strong wind a spark carries far.
- **Bring animals in**: dogs, poultry and livestock into a closed space, hives secured.

## Prepare for a power cut

- **Charge phones, power banks**, laptops and battery lamps.
- Keep a **torch, candles and matches** at hand, and a battery radio if you have one.
- **Store water**: a few bottles of drinking water and buckets for washing. Without electricity, pumps and hydrophores stop.
- If you heat with electricity, get **warm blankets** ready, and **food that needs no cooking**.
- Write down important phone numbers on paper.

## During the storm

- **Stay indoors** and away from windows. Do not set off on the road unless you must, especially through the forest over Fruška Gora.
- **Do not stand under trees**, power lines or near walls and fences in poor condition.
- **Do not touch fallen wires**, keep well away from them and report them to Elektrodistribucija (numbers below).
- Check on older neighbours who live alone.

## Emergency numbers

- **112** — single emergency number
- **192** — police, **193** — firefighters and rescue, **194** — ambulance
- **Power cuts and fallen wires**: Elektrodistribucija Srbije, Novi Sad branch — [021 421 066](tel:+38121421066), [021 421 068](tel:+38121421068), [0800 360 300](tel:0800360300) (free national number)

All useful numbers are on the [Useful information]({{< relref "useful" >}}) page.

Follow the warnings of the RHMZ and the Ministry of the Interior (MUP), and the instructions of the authorities.
