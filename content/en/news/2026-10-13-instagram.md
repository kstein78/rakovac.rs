The site now has its own Instagram account: **[@rakovac_rs](https://www.instagram.com/rakovac_rs/)**.

What you will find there:

- the best photos of Rakovac, the monastery and Fruška Gora: ours and those that neighbours share with us;
- local news from the village and around it;
- new pages and photo albums on this site.

Posts are in Serbian, Russian and English. The full stories, maps and practical details stay here on rakovac.rs; the link is in the profile.

Have a good photo of Rakovac? Send it in a direct message on Instagram or by [email](mailto:konstantin.stein@gmail.com). We publish photos only with the author's permission and always with their name.
