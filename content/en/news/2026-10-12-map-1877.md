The author of this site bought an original sheet of the **special map of the Kingdom of Hungary** at 1:144,000, edition 1877, folded in a cloth cover labelled in calligraphy **"Neusatz und Peterwardein"**. It was engraved in copper by the Military Geographical Institute in Vienna and sold for 70 kreuzer.

We have scanned it, joined the two halves and published the whole sheet: you can zoom in on Rakovac with the monastery sign above the village, the hill Gradac at 248 Viennese fathoms (about 470 m), and a road along the ridge called "Eugens Weg".

Novi Rakovac is not on the map yet: in 1877 the village was only the old settlement around the monastery.
