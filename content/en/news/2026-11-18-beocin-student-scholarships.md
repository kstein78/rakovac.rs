The scholarship commission of the municipality of Beočin has announced a call for student scholarships for the 2026/2027 academic year. Students from Rakovac can apply too: the village belongs to Beočin municipality.

## How much

- **120,000 dinars** per student for the year, paid in **ten equal monthly instalments of 12,000 dinars**.
- Payments run from 1 December 2026 to 30 November 2027, with no payments in July and August.

## Who can apply

- Students from the **second year** of undergraduate studies on, and master's and doctoral students.
- Studying on a **budget-funded place** at an institution founded by the Republic of Serbia or the Province of Vojvodina.
- Average grade of at least **8.5**, no repeated year, all exams from previous years passed.
- At least **three years** of residence in Beočin municipality.

Applications from students with disabilities or a chronic illness are assessed under somewhat more lenient criteria. A separate call has been announced for Roma students.

## When and where

Applications are accepted **from 12 to 26 November 2026**. The list of documents and the way to submit them are in the official call on the [municipality's website](https://www.beocin.rs/sr/?nav=table_rightad&id=1). The newspapers that reported the call do not give these details, so check the call itself or ask the municipality: Svetosavska 25, Beočin, [021 2970 260](tel:+381212970260).
