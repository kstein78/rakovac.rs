{{< note title="Not an official coat of arms" >}}The shield R is the identity of the rakovac.rs portal and was designed for it. It is not the coat of arms of the village or of the Municipality of Beočin. Using it does not mean that the municipality or rakovac.rs endorses a business.{{< /note >}}

## The logo

{{< identity-kit logos >}}

- The logo is the letter R in a shield, taken from the Rakovac Capital font. The rest of the name is set in Rakovac Common.
- For your own business, use the same system: the first letter of its name in Rakovac Capital, the rest in Rakovac Common.
- Leave free space around the shield of at least half its width on every side.
- Smallest size: the shield 12 mm high in print, 24 px on screen.
- Use the colour version on light backgrounds. On dark backgrounds, photos and glass, use the one-colour version in white or cream.
- Do not stretch, slant, outline or recolour the shield, and do not add shadows.

## Fonts

- **Rakovac Capital** only for the first letter of a name, title or paragraph, at the same size as the text. Smallest size: 24 pt (32 px) in colour, 14 pt (20 px) in one colour.
- **Rakovac Common** for everything else: all weights from Light to Black, with italics. It has Latin, Serbian and Russian Cyrillic, and the German and Hungarian letters.
- Colour for screens and full-colour print; one colour for vinyl stickers, stamps, embroidery and engraving.
- Canva and Google Docs cannot show colour fonts: place the SVG letters from the download as images there.

## Colours

{{< identity-kit colours >}}

Forest is the main colour: the shield, headings and dark backgrounds. Vine is for leaves and accents, Paper for light backgrounds and Ink for text. Use Grape sparingly, for the one thing that has to stand out.
