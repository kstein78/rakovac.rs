## What is in the download

- **Rakovac Capital Color** and the one-colour **Rakovac Capital**: Latin, Serbian and Russian Cyrillic, with Hungarian and German accents. Install them on Windows or macOS.
- **Rakovac Common**: the text font, all weights from Light to Black, with italics.
- Every capital as an SVG graphic, in colour and one colour, for print shops, Canva and sticker cutting.
- Web fonts with a ready style sheet, and the font guide in English and Serbian (PDF).

## How to use them

- Use the Capital only for the first letter of a name, title or paragraph, at the same size as the text.
- Colour for screens and full-colour print; one colour for vinyl stickers, stamps, embroidery and engraving.
- Canva and Google Docs cannot show colour fonts: use the SVG letters as images there.
