Novi Sad is the city you look down on from the hills above Rakovac, for example from the [swing lookout]({{< relref "nature/swing-lookout" >}}): the bridges, the tower blocks and, in the evening, its lights. Our neighbour Peter Perlin took these photos down there, in the city itself.
