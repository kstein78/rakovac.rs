Photos by Konstantin Stein from a morning walk in November 2026 up the hill above Stošin Do, the part of the village on the slope above Stari Rakovac.

In the morning the grass and the bramble leaves were white with hoarfrost. From the top the view opens two ways: down the valley onto Stari Rakovac and the bell tower of [Rakovac Monastery]({{< relref "monastery" >}}), and out through a gap in the woods to Novi Sad, with the arch of the Žeželj Bridge. More views from the hills: [Viewpoints around Rakovac]({{< relref "nature/viewpoints" >}}).
