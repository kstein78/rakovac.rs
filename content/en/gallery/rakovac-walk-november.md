Photos by our neighbour from Novi Rakovac, Ilya Vasilyev, from a walk through the village on 17 November 2026, in the order he took them.

On the way: the ruins of the medieval abbey at [Gradina]({{< relref "history/gradina-dombo" >}}), the school, the post office and the bus stop on the main road, [Mikromarket]({{< relref "shops/mikromarket" >}}), a house with the year 1912 on its gable, and the hillsides of the village with a view down to the Danube and the bridges of Novi Sad.
