Photos by Ilya Vasilyev (spring and summer) and Konstantin Stein (autumn), all taken from the same place on Belegirska Street in Novi Rakovac, looking south across the valley towards Stari Rakovac.

The wooded hilltop in the middle of the view is **Čardak** (*Čardak brdo*); the Yugoslav army map of 1984 gives it a height of 320 m ([old maps]({{< relref "history/maps" >}})). The white house with a red roof on the slope below is the **Lovački dom**, the hunting lodge. Thanks to Ilya Vasilyev for the names.
