**Beli Majdan** ("white quarry") is known locally as **Rakovačka pećina**, the Rakovac Cave. It is not a natural cave. It is an old quarry cut into the hillside, where stone was extracted until the 1930s. The stone was used for gravestones and house foundations, and most likely for Rakovac Monastery itself.

## What you will see

The entrance is several dozen metres wide and the hall runs about as deep into the hill. Its ceiling is 3 to 4 metres high and rests on thick stone pillars that the quarrymen left in place to keep the roof from falling. Tool marks and the outlines of cut blocks are still visible on the walls.

Steps cut into the rock lead up above the hall, to a spot with a view over the forest.

Hikers often mention a small opening in the ground nearby that breathes out warm air, most noticeable on cold days.

## How to get there

1. Drive or take bus 77 to **Rakovac Monastery** in Stari Rakovac.
2. On the road next to the monastery, about 100 m from the gate, a sign reads **"Beli Majdan"**.
3. Follow the steep earth track uphill. It takes about 5 to 10 minutes.

From Beli Majdan a marked path continues into the forest to the [Hermitage of Isaija Parivodski]({{< relref "nature/isposnica" >}}) (about 40 minutes) and up to the Gradac peaks.

## A local legend

Like any mysterious place, Beli Majdan has its tale: a cave-dweller called **Veliki Sremac Baća** is said to live deep inside. Nobody has ever produced a photograph.
