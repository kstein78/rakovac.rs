Forests cover about 90 per cent of the [National Park](https://npfruskagora.co.rs/en/plants): cool mixed woods of sessile oak and hornbeam, beech on the shaded slopes, warm forests of Turkey and Hungarian oak, and a large share of silver linden. On the sunny edges grow the remains of the old Pannonian steppe. The figure of more than 2,000 larger fungi comes from Eleonora Čapelja, president of the Novi Sad Mushroom Society ([Telegraf](https://www.telegraf.rs/vesti/srbija/3668480-fruska-gora-prepuna-gljiva)).

## Trees

{{< atlas >}}
{{< species icon="lindenleaf" latin="Tilia tomentosa" name="Silver linden" >}}The tree that gives the forests of Fruška Gora their character: silvery-white undersides to the leaves and fragrant blossom in early summer.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="oakleaf" latin="Quercus petraea" name="Sessile oak" >}}With hornbeam it forms the cooler mixed forests on the slopes.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="oakleaf" latin="Quercus cerris" name="Turkey oak" >}}An oak of the warm, dry slopes; its acorn cups are covered in long, curly scales.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="oakleaf" latin="Quercus frainetto" name="Hungarian oak" >}}An oak with large, deeply lobed leaves that grows with the Turkey oak in the warm forests.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="tree" latin="Fagus sylvatica" name="European beech" >}}Grows on the cooler, shaded slopes; smooth grey bark and a deep, dark shade in summer.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="tree" latin="Carpinus betulus" name="Hornbeam" >}}A smooth, fluted grey trunk, as if made of muscle; a common companion of the sessile oak.{{< /species >}}

## Flowers of the steppe slopes

{{< atlas >}}
{{< species icon="flower" latin="Dictamnus albus" level="caution" name="Burning bush" >}}A tall, fragrant plant of the sunny steppe slopes with pink-veined flowers. Do not touch it on sunny days: its oils, together with sunlight, can cause burn-like blisters on the skin.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="flower" latin="Adonis vernalis" level="poison" name="Spring pheasant's eye" >}}Large yellow flowers on the steppe slopes in early spring, a relic of the ancient steppe. Poisonous to people and animals; leave it where it grows.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="iris" latin="Iris pumila" name="Dwarf iris" >}}A tiny iris of the dry grassland, flowering in spring in violet or yellow; part of the relict steppe vegetation.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="grass" latin="Stipa pulcherrima" name="Feather grass" >}}The silky, feather-like awns of this steppe grass wave in the wind on the sunny slopes in early summer.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="flower" latin="Orchidaceae" level="protected" name="Orchids" >}}Orchids are among the plant families with the most species in the National Park. Admire and photograph them, but never pick or dig them up.{{< /species >}}
{{< /atlas >}}

## Mushrooms: read this first

{{< note type="warning" title="Never eat a mushroom you are not completely sure of" >}}Edible and poisonous mushrooms can look alike, and there is no rule of thumb that tells them apart: no silver spoon, no peeling test, no "the snails ate it". Have every mushroom checked by an experienced picker or by the Novi Sad Mushroom Society (Gljivarsko društvo Novi Sad). The drawings on this page are decorative and must not be used for identification.{{< /note >}}

### Mushrooms people pick (only after an expert's check)

{{< atlas >}}
{{< species icon="morel" latin="Morchella" name="Morel" >}}One of the first mushrooms of the year, appearing soon after the snow melts. Lookalike: the poisonous false morel (Gyromitra), with a brain-like cap.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="bracket" latin="Laetiporus sulphureus" name="Chicken of the woods" >}}Bright yellow-orange shelves on tree trunks, from May onwards.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="chanterelle" latin="Cantharellus cibarius" name="Chanterelle" >}}Egg-yellow, with forked ridges instead of true gills, growing from the ground. Lookalike: the poisonous jack-o'-lantern (see below).{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="bracket" latin="Fistulina hepatica" name="Beefsteak fungus" >}}A liver-red bracket on old oaks that oozes red juice when cut.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="mushroom" latin="Macrolepiota procera" name="Parasol mushroom" >}}A tall mushroom with a snakeskin-patterned stem and a ring that slides up and down. Smaller, poisonous parasols look similar.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="mushroom" latin="Boletus edulis" name="Porcini (cep)" >}}Brown cap, pale pores instead of gills and a thick stem. It grows on Fruška Gora too, but in smaller numbers.{{< /species >}}
{{< /atlas >}}

### Poisonous and deadly

{{< atlas >}}
{{< species icon="amanita" latin="Amanita phalloides" level="deadly" name="Death cap" >}}Responsible for most fatal mushroom poisonings worldwide. Greenish-olive cap, white gills, a ring on the stem and a sack-like volva at the base. A single cap can kill, and symptoms start only after about 12 hours.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="amanita" latin="Amanita verna, A. virosa" level="deadly" name="Destroying angels" >}}All-white relatives of the death cap and just as deadly. Easily mistaken for young white field mushrooms: check for the volva at the base.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="chanterelle" latin="Omphalotus olearius" level="poison" name="Jack-o'-lantern" >}}Orange like a chanterelle, but grows in clusters on wood and has true, crowded gills. Causes vomiting and diarrhoea.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="flyagaric" latin="Amanita muscaria" level="poison" name="Fly agaric" >}}The fairy-tale red mushroom with white spots. Poisonous: never eat it.{{< /species >}}
{{< /atlas >}}

## If someone may be poisoned

- Go to hospital at once, or call **194**. Do not wait for symptoms to pass and do not treat yourself.
- With the death cap, symptoms start only about 12 hours after the meal; treatment within the first 48 hours greatly improves the chances (Military Medical Academy, quoted by [BBC News Serbian](https://www.danas.rs/bbc-news-serbian/srbija-trovanje-pecurkama-na-sta-treba-da-pazite-kada-berete-gljive-a-sta-se-desava-ako-se-otrujete/)).
