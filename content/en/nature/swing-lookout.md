The place has no sign, so on this site we simply call it the lookout with the swing. In a straight line it is about 1.1 km south of the [Gradina archaeological site]({{< relref "history/gradina-dombo" >}}) and about 1.4 km from the [monastery]({{< relref "monastery" >}}).

To the north the view opens over the Danube and Novi Sad; on clear days you can pick out the city's bridges and tower blocks. Summer storms can be watched coming across the plain long before they reach the hill, and in late autumn the valley fills with haze. The photos below were all taken from this one meadow in different seasons.

The swing hangs from a tree above a steep slope. Hold small children, and check the rope before you sit down.
