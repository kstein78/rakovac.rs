Fruška Gora is a low, isolated mountain rising from the Pannonian plain. It was the first national park in Serbia, and its highest point, Crveni čot, reaches 538 m. The northern slopes above Rakovac are covered in deciduous forest; lower down are orchards, vineyards and weekend houses.

Most walks start at **Rakovac Monastery** in Stari Rakovac, where there is room to leave a car and where city bus 77 from Novi Sad stops. Trails are marked with painted signs on trees and poles. Some are steep and muddy after rain, so good shoes make a real difference.

{{< note title="Before you set off" >}}
- The area is a national park: no camping and no open fires outside official picnic sites.
- Mobile coverage is patchy in the valleys. Download an offline map before you go.
- In summer bring water and insect repellent; hikers regularly mention mosquitoes near the streams.
- Emergency number: **112** (ambulance **194**).
