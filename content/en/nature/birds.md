Fruška Gora is a national park, and its forests cover about 90 per cent of it. The National Park counts [220 bird species](https://npfruskagora.co.rs/en/animals); the birding guide [Birding Places](https://www.birdingplaces.eu/es/birdingplaces/serbia/fruska-gora-national-park) speaks of about 150 in the park itself and about 210 with the lower slopes. Rakovac lies right on that edge between forest, orchards and vineyards.

## Birdwatching near Rakovac

- **When:** spring and autumn migration are the best seasons, and May and June when the forest birds sing and nest. Go out early in the morning; owls are heard in the evening.
- **Where:** the forest edge above the monastery and the path to [Beli Majdan]({{< relref "nature/beli-majdan" >}}); the meadows at the [lookouts]({{< relref "nature/swing-lookout" >}}) for birds of prey; the orchards and vineyards of the lower slopes for buntings and shrikes; and the Danube floodplain below the village.
- **How:** binoculars, quiet and patience. Stay on the paths and keep away from nests: all these species are protected. You can record what you see in eBird, which helps researchers.

## Birds of prey and the black stork

{{< atlas >}}
{{< species icon="eagle" latin="Aquila heliaca" level="rare" name="Eastern imperial eagle" >}}The symbol of Fruška Gora and its rarest bird. Sources disagree on whether it still nests here: the National Park speaks of about four pairs, while conservationists say it has disappeared as a breeding bird. A sighting is worth reporting to the National Park.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="stork" latin="Ciconia nigra" name="Black stork" >}}The shy forest cousin of the white stork: it nests deep in the woods and feeds along streams. Black, with a red bill and red legs.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="eagle" latin="Circaetus gallicus" name="Short-toed snake eagle" >}}A large, pale eagle that hovers over clearings and hunts snakes and lizards. A summer visitor, here from spring to autumn.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="eagle" latin="Hieraaetus pennatus" name="Booted eagle" >}}Europe's smallest eagle, about the size of a buzzard. It nests on Fruška Gora and spends the winter in Africa.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="eagle" latin="Buteo buteo" name="Common buzzard" >}}The bird of prey you are most likely to see above Rakovac: it circles over the fields with a mewing call.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="eagle" latin="Falco peregrinus" name="Peregrine falcon" >}}The fastest bird on Earth in a dive. Fruška Gora is its only breeding site in Vojvodina.{{< /species >}}
{{< /atlas >}}

## Woodpeckers and owls

{{< atlas >}}
{{< species icon="woodpecker" latin="Dryocopus martius" name="Black woodpecker" >}}Europe's largest woodpecker: all black with a red crown. In old beech and oak woods you hear its loud drumming and ringing call from far away.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="woodpecker" latin="Dendrocoptes medius" name="Middle spotted woodpecker" >}}A woodpecker with an all-red crown, tied to old oaks. It appears on the National Park's list of notable birds.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="woodpecker" latin="Dendrocopos major" name="Great spotted woodpecker" >}}The commonest woodpecker in the woods and orchards around the village: black and white, with a red patch under the tail.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="owl" latin="Strix aluco" name="Tawny owl" >}}The owl of old forests and parks. Its hooting carries far on autumn and winter nights.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="owl" latin="Otus scops" name="Eurasian scops owl" >}}A tiny owl with ear tufts that comes from Africa for the summer. On warm nights it repeats one short, soft whistle for hours.{{< /species >}}
{{< /atlas >}}

## Songbirds

{{< atlas >}}
{{< species icon="songbird" latin="Ficedula albicollis" name="Collared flycatcher" >}}A small black-and-white bird with a white collar that nests in holes in old trees.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="songbird" latin="Phylloscopus sibilatrix" name="Wood warbler" >}}A yellow-green warbler of the beech woods; its song sounds like a coin spinning on a plate.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="songbird" latin="Emberiza hortulana" name="Ortolan bunting" >}}A bird of warm slopes, vineyards and orchards, and one of the species birdwatchers come to Fruška Gora for.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="songbird" latin="Lanius collurio" name="Red-backed shrike" >}}A bird of hedges and pastures that impales its prey on thorns. The male wears a black mask across the eyes.{{< /species >}}
{{< species icon="songbird" latin="Coccothraustes coccothraustes" name="Hawfinch" >}}A big finch with a massive bill strong enough to crack cherry stones.{{< /species >}}
