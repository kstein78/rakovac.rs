Stražilovo is a wooded valley on the north-eastern slopes of Fruška Gora, a few kilometres from Sremski Karlovci and about 15 km from Novi Sad. It holds the grave and monument of the Romantic poet **Branko Radičević**. There is also a mountaineers' hut, and it is the starting point of the **Fruška Gora transversal**, a hiking trail of more than 160 km around the mountain.

It is not in Rakovac, but it makes a good day out from a stay here.
