An *isposnica* is a hermitage: a place where a monk withdrew from the monastery to live alone, fast and pray. This one is carved into soft, light limestone on a small plateau below a cliff. The entrance is set slightly to the side and is less than 1.5 m high. A narrow window lets in a little light.

It is the **only hermitage of its kind in Vojvodina** and is protected as a cultural monument. It is believed to have been made by monks of Rakovac Monastery in the 18th century.

## The inscriptions

The rock around the entrance is covered with names and dates carved over several centuries, many in old church script, alongside plenty of modern signatures. The oldest inscription bears the name of **Isaija Parivodski**, after whom the hermitage is named. Published sources disagree about its date (1711 or 1751).

## How to get there

- **From Rakovac Monastery:** walk up to [Beli Majdan]({{< relref "nature/beli-majdan" >}}), then follow the marked path further into the forest, about 40 minutes. The path is steep in places.
- **From Stari Ledinci:** a second path leads up from the Ledinci side.

Wear proper shoes, and in summer protect yourself against mosquitoes. Camping and fires are not allowed.
