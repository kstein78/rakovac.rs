Kesten ("chestnut") is a hill east of the monastery. The viewpoint looks out over Novi Sad and the southern plain of Bačka. The walk from Rakovac or from Stari Ledinci passes the Hermitage of Isaija Parivodski and the Neven spring.

{{< note type="warning" title="Access may have changed" >}}In 2021 local residents reported that fences and gates near Kesten hill had cut the old yellow-marked trail between Novi Rakovac and Zmajevac, as well as the path from the monastery towards the Kesten spring. Check locally before you go.{{< /note >}}
