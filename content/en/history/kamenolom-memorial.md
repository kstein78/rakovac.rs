About 500 m from the monastery, beside the road up to Zmajevac, stands the memorial complex known as **Kamenolom** ("the quarry"). It marks the place where in September 1941 residents of Rakovac and workers of the quarry swore the partisan oath.

Large plaques surround a central open space and carry the names of fighters who fell and of the civilian victims from Rakovac. Part of the complex is built into the hillside and looks like the entrance to a mine gallery. The introductory plaque is dated **1941–1961**, so the memorial was most likely unveiled in 1961.

A mosaic with the words *"Druže Tito, mi ti se kunemo"* ("Comrade Tito, we swear to you") was painted over in the 1990s and is visible again today.
