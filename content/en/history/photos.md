## How the archive is organised

Photos are sorted by year and grouped by decade. Where the year is not known exactly, it is marked as approximate. Each photo shows who took it, if that is known, and who shared it. You can filter the archive by decade or by photographer.

A photo stays in the archive only as long as the person who shared it agrees. Write to us and we will correct a caption or remove a picture.
