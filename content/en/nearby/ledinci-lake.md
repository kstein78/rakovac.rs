Hidden in the forest above Stari Ledinci lies **Lake Ledinci**, a turquoise lake that filled an abandoned stone quarry around 1999. It is about 15 minutes from Rakovac by car, and forest paths lead towards it on foot as well.

{{< note type="warning" title="Currently closed" >}}The lake is not open to visitors. In May 2025 the quarry company that holds the site announced a ban on all visits because of reclamation works, and in August 2026 a guard on site was still turning visitors away from the water. Swimming is dangerous here: the walls are almost vertical, rockfalls are frequent and the lake is about 95 m deep. In 2006 a rockfall injured several swimmers. Please respect the ban.{{< /note >}}

A small detail for history lovers: the company that owns the site is called **Kamenolom Rakovac**, the "Rakovac quarry". Its records from 1929–1955 are kept at the Archive of Vojvodina.

For swimming, especially with small children, go to [Lake Bešenovo]({{< relref "nearby/besenovo-lake" >}}) instead: it has shallow, gently sloping places along the shore.
