**Vrdnik** is a spa village on the southern slopes of Fruška Gora. From Rakovac the road climbs past the monastery to Zmajevac and drops down the other side: about 12 minutes by car. Above the village stands the medieval [Vrdnik Tower]({{< relref "nearby/vrdnicka-kula" >}}).

## Fruške Terme

The **Fruške Terme** thermal resort (today run as Mövenpick Resort & Spa Fruške Terme) is the biggest attraction. The hotel was built in 2018–2020 and the outdoor pools in 2021–2022. There are **12 pools**, indoor and outdoor, with about 2,500 m² of water at 28–33 °C, including a panoramic pool and a glass-walled pool. The resort is designed for about 500 overnight guests and 800 day visitors.

Kosta's tip: in his view it is the best thermal spa hotel in Serbia.

## Termal

In the centre of Vrdnik, the older **Termal** spa hotel has indoor thermal pools that are also open to day visitors.

## Where to eat: Konak Stari Orah

Vrdnik has plenty of small restaurants. Kosta's favourite is **Konak Stari Orah** (Fruškogorska 17, phone [+381 22 465770](tel:+38122465770)): traditional Serbian cooking with smoked specialities, seating for about 100 guests including a garden. It is still open according to its current map listing.
