Dates change from year to year: check with the organisers before you go; we post them in the [news]({{< relref "news" >}}). Driving times are approximate, from Stari Rakovac, calculated from OpenStreetMap data.

**EXIT** is no longer held at the Petrovaradin Fortress: after the 2025 edition the festival left the fortress and in 2026 goes on a world tour ([DJ Mag](https://djmag.com/news/exit-festival-announces-2026-global-tour-following-departure-serbia)).

For Vrdnik itself we found no village festival of its own apart from Vidovdan at the monastery and the opening of Tour de Fruška. Know a kirbaj, a slava or a fair we have missed, in Rakovac or nearby? Write to us.
