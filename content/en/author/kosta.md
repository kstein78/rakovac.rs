I came to Rakovac in June 2023. We live in Stari Rakovac with my wife and our two daughters: the elder was born in 2020, the younger in 2025, already here in Serbia. Contacts are on the [About the author]({{< relref "author" >}}) page.

## Family and animals

Six cats and three dogs live with us. Three of them came with us from Russia: Simba the sphynx cat, Puša, a grey fluffy cat who is 17, and Ralf the dog. Simba and Ralf are friends, and not at all the way cats and dogs usually are. Our second dog is Zoya, who is black. The third has owners somewhere in the neighbourhood, but comes to live with us for weeks at a time.

## Where I come from

I was born in Kharkiv. For many years I led large digital media; today I work on creative projects, promotion and new projects built with the help of artificial intelligence. My mother is a chemist, and for almost 15 years I spent every week in her laboratory, where she analysed harmful substances in the environment. That is why clean air matters so much to me. I speak Serbian, Russian, English and German.

## What I do for Rakovac

- **This website.** [rakovac.rs]({{< relref "/" >}}) belongs to everyone who lives here: anyone can send news, an old photo or a correction.
- **The Russian-speaking chat of Rakovac.** I started it, so that Russian-speaking neighbours get important news and can agree on things together.
- **Air.** Since spring 2026 I have been writing to the municipality, the Ministry and the Province about the chemical smell over the village, have met the municipality's environmental office, and with neighbours I am working on a certified air sensor for the municipality (more below).
- **Water cuts.** In 2026 I went to JKP Beočin, the water plant and the municipality, and passed the news about water tankers to Russian-speaking and Serbian neighbours.

## What I would like us to solve together

I believe we all share the same worries. Together with other Russian-speaking neighbours, these are the ones I raise:

- **Air.** Since spring 2024 our family has noticed a sharp chemical smell several times a week, mostly early in the morning. We do not remember it in 2023, but the problem has probably been going on much longer. And not only in Rakovac and Beočin: Ledinci, Bocke, Sremska Kamenica and the surrounding area smell it too. Since spring 2026 I have been writing to the Beočin municipality, the Ministry of Environmental Protection and the Province and getting answers. With Russian-speaking neighbours from Rakovac and Beočin we agreed to buy a measuring device and give it to the municipality. I spent a long time looking for a sensor that can be used officially in Serbia; it turned out to cost about 10,000 euros, too much for a few families. My proposal: a certified sensor for a broad air analysis, bought together with the neighbouring villages and the suburbs of Novi Sad, so that the municipality cannot ignore its readings. In the Viber group "Alarm za vazduh" people regularly report bad air and almost unanimously name the Beočin cement plant as the cause; I believe they will support it. I also ask for an inspection of waste burning at the cement plant, especially between 4 and 8 a.m., and for the municipality's air-quality plans to be made public.
- **Water.** Since 2024 Stari Rakovac is left without water every summer; in August 2026, for 24–48 hours at a time. My proposals, sent to the municipality and JKP Beočin on 6 August 2026: a pump and pressure tank for Stari Rakovac; the year-round stream above the village as an extra source, after testing; notice of cuts 24 hours ahead and not only on Facebook; a regular drinking-water tanker while the cuts last.
- **Buses.** Line 77 is the only direct link from Stari Rakovac to Novi Sad: on weekdays it leaves 5 times (05:20, 06:25, 12:30, 15:25, 17:05), on Saturdays 3 times, on Sundays 4. Line 78 goes only to Beočin, with a change there. On weekdays there is no bus to town between 06:25 and 12:30, more than six hours; on Saturdays between 07:40 and 15:15, on Sundays between 04:45 and 11:55. The walk to the junction takes 30–40 minutes, which is hard for older people and mothers with children ([timetable]({{< relref "useful" >}}#bus)).
- **Playgrounds.** Rakovac has practically no proper children's playground today: the one next to the monastery has been removed, and only a small, neglected one is left by the Kamenolom memorial ([playgrounds]({{< relref "kids/playgrounds" >}})). To play outside with our children we drive up to the ridge, to Beočin, Vrdnik or Sremska Kamenica.
- **Sidewalks.** Stošin Do has no sidewalk, so walking there with children is dangerous, especially in winter on ice or after dark, and now it is dark by about 4 p.m. On Manastirska street, at the junction with Stošin Do, you have to run across the road on a bend to reach the sidewalk, and towards the monastery twice more, because the sidewalk ends on one side of the road and starts on the other, with no pedestrian crossings. With a pram and two children every such walk is hard.
- **Separate waste.** There is no container for plastic in Rakovac, so we drive it to Beočin. If JKP collected plastic here, we would chip in for a container.
- **Clean transport.** I drive only an electric car and am happy to advise neighbours about electric vehicles: charging, costs, servicing.
