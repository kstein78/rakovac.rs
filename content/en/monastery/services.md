## Service times

| Service | Days | Starts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Divine Liturgy | weekdays and Saturdays | 7:00 |
| Divine Liturgy | Sundays and church feast days | 8:00 |
| Vespers | every day, weekends included | 16:00 |

Times as given by the [Novi Sad Tourist Organisation](https://novisad.travel/manastiri-single/m%D0%B0n%D0%B0stir-r%D0%B0kov%D0%B0c/) (page updated in April 2024). Before a major feast or a long trip, check by phone: [+381 21 6275 022](tel:+381216275022).

The Serbian Orthodox Church follows the Julian calendar, so church feasts fall 13 days later than in the civil calendar: Christmas is on 7 January.

## Visiting the monastery

- **Opening hours:** 7:00 to 19:30 in summer, 7:00 to 17:00 in winter.
- **Groups** should announce their visit two days ahead by email; the address is on the [Tourist Organisation's page](https://novisad.travel/manastiri-single/m%D0%B0n%D0%B0stir-r%D0%B0kov%D0%B0c/).
- **Dress:** modest, shoulders and knees covered.
- **Getting there:** along Manastirska street from Novi Rakovac, or by GSP Novi Sad bus, lines 77 and 78 (trips marked SR). See the [timetable]({{< relref "useful" >}}#bus).
