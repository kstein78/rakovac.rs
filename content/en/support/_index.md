## Free listing: for everyone in Rakovac

Every restaurant, host, shop, farm and producer in Rakovac can have a page on this site at no cost:

- name, a short description and a photo;
- address, phone, website and a **Get directions** button;
- a place on the site map and in the right section (Stay, Eat & drink, Local products…).

## Supporter: help the project grow

Businesses that make a voluntary donation to the project become **Supporters of rakovac.rs**. In return they get:

- an extended page with **professional photos** taken by our team;
- a **short video feature** on the page (and on the rakovac.rs YouTube channel);
- a **Supporter** badge and a place at the top of their section;
- mentions in walking-route descriptions and seasonal articles (for example "where to eat after the waterfall walk");
- **cross-promotion**: we share their news, and they display a small rakovac.rs QR sign for guests.

Donations go to hosting, photography, filming and translation into five languages.

## Cross-promotion

We welcome a link back to rakovac.rs from your website or booking page, and a QR sign in your guest house or restaurant. We will make the sign for you.

## Get in touch

Write to **hello@rakovac.rs**. Tell us your business name, what you offer and how to reach you.
