Bike Park Rakovac (BPR) is a mountain bike park in Stari Rakovac, near [the monastery]({{< relref "monastery" >}}), run by the non-profit association NVO Bike Park Rakovac with volunteers. Entry is free and the trails are open to everyone.

## Trails

- **Zmajev trag** (Dragon Tail), beginners: a flowing forest trail, about 1,400 m.
- **Junior**, intermediate: under construction.
- **Viktor**, advanced.
- **Juliška DH**, downhill for experienced riders only, cut into the rock of [Beli Majdan]({{< relref "nature/beli-majdan" >}}).

## Shuttle and prices

The BPR shuttle takes riders and bikes uphill every day: half day (4 hours) **2,000 dinars**, full day (8 hours) **3,000 dinars**. Departures in summer from 10:00 and from 14:00, in winter 9:00–13:00 and 13:00 until sunset. It runs when at least 5 riders have booked. [Book online](https://www.bikepark-rakovac.rs/rezervacije); bookings close 36 hours before, changes and cancellations up to 24 hours before. The park can also arrange transport from Novi Sad for up to 7 people with bikes.

## Bikes and safety

There are no rental bikes at the moment; one free loaner bike and a helmet can be borrowed if you ask in advance. A mountain bike helmet is mandatory; gloves and pads are recommended. Every rider signs the [terms of use](https://www.bikepark-rakovac.rs/dokumenti/uslovi-koriscenja-sr.pdf).
