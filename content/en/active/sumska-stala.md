Šumska štala ("Forest Stable") is a riding club in Stari Ledinci, the neighbouring village east of Rakovac. It offers trail rides through the Fruška Gora forest, a riding school for beginners and experienced riders, and a summer riding camp for children.

## Trail rides

| Ride | Duration | Per person |
|---|---|---|
| Zeleno srce | 30 min, 2 km | 3,000 din |
| Dva vidikovca | 1 h 40 min, 5.5 km | 5,000 din |
| Staza orlova | 2 h 10 min, 7 km | 6,000 din |
| Šumska staza | 1 h 40 min, 5 km | 6,500 din |

Beginners get a short training first, and a guide goes along. Šumska staza is only for experienced riders, at the head instructor's discretion. In bad weather a shorter ride to the lake replaces Dva vidikovca and Staza orlova, for 4,500 dinars. Book at least one day ahead, several days for weekends. Gift vouchers are valid for 3 months.

Contacts: [sumskastala@gmail.com](mailto:sumskastala@gmail.com), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/sumskastala/). Prices from the [club's website](https://sumskastala.rs/terensko-jahanje/), checked on 10 November 2026.
