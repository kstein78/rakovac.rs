Konjički klub Mambo is a riding club on the edge of Beočin, at the foot of Fruška Gora. According to the [Novi Sad tourist organisation](https://novisad.travel/en/jahanje-konja), it runs a riding school mainly for children and walks with horses for the youngest, and has a café by the training ground.

Prices are not published: ask by phone or on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/konjicki_klub_mambo/).
