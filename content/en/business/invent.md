Founded in **1986**, Invent develops and makes sanitary fittings: **instant water-heater taps** (taps with a built-in heater), **toilet cisterns**, single-lever mixer taps, valves and siphons. Its customers are distributors, plumbers and designers across Serbia, especially in Vojvodina and Belgrade.
