Did you know? People lived in Rakovac about 7,000 years ago, long before there were towns or roads. Read [their story]({{< relref "kids/story" >}}), then go out and look for the [five treasures of Rakovac]({{< relref "kids/quest" >}}).

{{< note title="For parents" >}}Every walk on these pages is short, or clearly marked as longer. The forest paths are steep and muddy after rain and are not made for prams, so bring proper shoes, water and, in summer, something against mosquitoes. Check for ticks after walks in spring and summer.{{< /note >}}
