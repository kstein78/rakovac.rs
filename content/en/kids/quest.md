Can you find all five? Tick each treasure when you find it: your ticks stay on this phone. You can also print the page and draw what you found in the boxes.

{{< quest >}}
1. **The stone pillars.** At Beli Majdan, the roof of a huge hall rests on pillars of stone that the quarrymen left standing. How many can you count from the entrance?
2. **The stonecutters' marks.** Look closely at the walls of Beli Majdan: you can still see the marks of the tools that cut the stone.
3. **The bell tower.** The monastery bell tower was built in 1735. Find it through the trees on your walk.
4. **The year in the rock.** At the [Hermitage]({{< relref "nature/isposnica" >}}), a monk called Isaija carved his name into the stone in 1771. Can you find a year carved in the rock? (A long walk: about 1 hour from the monastery.)
5. **The waterfall.** Walk to the [Dumbovac waterfall]({{< relref "nature/dumbovac-waterfall" >}}). Can you hear it before you see it? (For good walkers, 8 years and up.)
{{< /quest >}}

{{< note type="warning" title="For parents" >}}Look into Beli Majdan from the entrance only: going inside is allowed only with a national park ranger, because stones can fall. Hold small children by the hand on the steep track and on the steps above the hall.{{< /note >}}
