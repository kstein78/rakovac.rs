In Rakovac itself there is no place to swim, but two good options are less than half an hour away by car.

## Lake Bešenovo: shallow water by the shore

**About 25 minutes by car.** A big lake in an old quarry, with **gently sloping, shallow places** along the beach where small children can paddle and play. Since 2026 there are sun loungers and bars on the shore. There are no lifeguards and little shade: take hats, sun cream and water, stay with your children at the water's edge and don't swim out into the deep middle. On hot weekends the car park is full, so come early. [More about Lake Bešenovo]({{< relref "nearby/besenovo-lake" >}}).

## Fruške Terme in Vrdnik: warm pools all year

**About 12 minutes by car.** The thermal resort has indoor and outdoor pools with warm water and a **children's water play area** with sprays, a rainbow tunnel and a slide. It works in winter too. [More about Vrdnik and Fruške Terme]({{< relref "nearby/vrdnik-fruske-terme" >}}).

## Not for children: Lake Ledinci

[Lake Ledinci]({{< relref "nearby/ledinci-lake" >}}) is beautiful but closed to visitors, and the water is very deep right at the shore, with steep walls and falling rocks. Don't go there with children.

{{< note title="For parents" >}}Quarry lakes have no lifeguards and the bottom can drop away suddenly near the walls. Keep small children within arm's reach and give them armbands.{{< /note >}}
