Trails on Fruška Gora are marked with painted signs on trees and poles. The distances and climbs below come from published hiking reports. We will replace them with our own GPS measurements once we have walked each route with a camera.

{{< route id="beli-majdan" name="1. Monastery – Beli Majdan" distance="about 600 m one way" time="10 min" level="easy" start="45.184697,19.774143" >}}
The shortest walk, and fine with children. From the monastery follow the "Beli Majdan" sign up a steep earth track to the old quarry hall (entry into the gallery only with a park ranger). Climb the rock-cut steps above the hall for a view over the treetops. [More about Beli Majdan]({{< relref "nature/beli-majdan" >}}).
{{< /route >}}

{{< route id="hermitage-loop" name="2. Hermitage loop" distance="about 8 km, circular" level="moderate" start="45.184697,19.774143" >}}
Monastery – Hermitage of Isaija Parivodski – viewpoint – Plana – Izvidnica – Beli Majdan – Monastery. Almost the entire loop runs through forest. The hermitage is the highlight: a monk's cell cut into a limestone cliff. [More about the hermitage]({{< relref "nature/isposnica" >}}).
{{< /route >}}

{{< route id="dumbovac" name="3. Dumbovac waterfall" distance="about 3 km one way" time="about 1 h one way" climb="about 150 m" level="moderate" start="45.184697,19.774143" >}}
Walk up the asphalt road past the monastery. After about 600 m a marked path turns off towards the waterfall. Return the same way.

Experienced walkers can continue west to the **Šakotinac waterfall** and come down to the village of Gornji Šakotinac, about 10 km and 3 hours in total. It ends far from Rakovac, so arrange a lift or a taxi back. [More about the waterfall]({{< relref "nature/dumbovac-waterfall" >}}).
{{< /route >}}

{{< route id="gradac-zmajevac" name="4. Gradac peaks and Zmajevac" distance="about 15 km, circular" climb="about 500 m in total" level="hard" start="45.184697,19.774143" >}}
Monastery – Beli Majdan – Hermitage – the old Rakovac quarry – Mali and Veliki Gradac (470 m) – Zmajevac – back down to the monastery. From Beli Majdan the path to Gradac follows the blue markings and climbs steadily. At Zmajevac, the former mountaineers' hut is now a restaurant, a good place for a break.
{{< /route >}}

## Getting to the start by bus

City and suburban buses of GSP Novi Sad serve Rakovac:

- **Line 77** (towards Stari Rakovac) stops right by the monastery (stop "Stari Rakovac VII – manastir").
- **Line 78** (towards Beočin Selo): the runs marked **SR** go through Stari Rakovac and also stop at the monastery. The other runs stay on the main road and stop in Novi Rakovac; from the traffic lights at the main crossroads it is about 3 km on foot, south along the asphalt road, to the monastery.

In Novi Sad both lines leave from the intercity bus station (MAS, platforms 13 and 14). Departure times of both lines are in the [timetable]({{< relref "useful" >}}#bus).
