The fee has been charged since 2009 under Serbian law; the money goes to protecting the park and keeping trails and picnic areas in order. Until now the park's nature wardens collected it on the road, mostly at weekends. From **5 November 2026** vehicles are recorded electronically and the fee can be paid online ([news]({{< relref "news/2026-11-01-fruska-gora-park-fee" >}})).

## Prices

From the park's [2026 price list](https://fruskagoranovisajt.rs/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/cenovnik.pdf):

| | One day | Annual card |
|---|---|---|
| Adult on foot | 100 dinars | 600 dinars |
| Child on foot | 50 dinars | |
| Car or motorcycle | 300 dinars | 1,800 dinars |
| Van, vehicle with a trailer | 500 dinars | 3,300 dinars |
| Bus, truck | 800 dinars | 6,500 dinars |
| Articulated lorry, truck with trailer | 2,000 dinars | 18,000 dinars |

[Annual cards](https://fruskagoranovisajt.rs/godisnje-karte/) are valid for the calendar year, to 31 December. A one-hour ticket for a car, 100 dinars, has been reported for the electronic system; it is not in the official price list yet.

## Where to pay

- **Online:** [enaplatafruskagora.co.rs](https://enaplatafruskagora.co.rs/) (on 1 November 2026 it still said "portal in preparation") and the [ticket page](https://app.creativedimension.us/gora/korisnickaapp/karte_kupovina.php) linked from the [park's website](https://npfruskagora.co.rs/).
- **In person:** the [Information Centre on Iriški Venac](https://maps.app.goo.gl/MbSNVzZF94mnHa8y8), at the crossing of the main road over the mountain and the ridge road.
- **Annual cards** are also sold at the park's offices in Beočin, Vrdnik, Ležimir and Erdevik.

## Who does not pay

The [law](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon-o-naknadama-za-koriscenje-javnih-dobara.html) (article 109) exempts, among others, pre-school children, people with disabilities, and pensioners with the lowest pension (for the car, with the last pension slip). It also lists exemptions for people who live in the park's municipalities and for those who work in the park.

## Local residents and driving through

How the electronic system will treat local residents' cars, and whether you pay just for driving across Fruška Gora (for example Rakovac – Ruma), is not clear yet. The park has not published rules on this, nor how vehicles are recorded and what the fine is for not paying. Before you rely on anything, ask the park.
