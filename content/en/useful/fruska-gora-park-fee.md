The fee has been charged since 2009 under Serbian law; the money goes to protecting the park and keeping trails and picnic areas in order. Since **5 November 2026** cameras record vehicles in the park, recognise the category and calculate the fee ([news]({{< relref "news/2026-11-05-fruska-gora-residents-exempt" >}})).

## Prices

From the park's [fee decision](https://enaplatafruskagora.co.rs/Content/documents/fruskagora/Odluka-o-naknadama.pdf) (last amended on 30 December 2025):

| | 1 hour | Day | Year |
|---|---|---|---|
| I: car, motorcycle | 100 | 300 | 1,800 |
| II: van, with trailer | 150 | 500 | 3,300 |
| III: bus, truck | 200 | 800 | 6,500 |
| IV: over 3 axles | 300 | 2,000 | 18,000 |
| On foot: adult / child | | 100/50 | 600 |

Dinars. "1 hour" is a pass through the park of up to one hour. Category I is up to 1.3 m high; III is higher than 1.3 m.

Annual cards are valid for the calendar year, to 31 December. The fee board at the [Information Centre]({{< relref "nearby/fruska-gora-information-centre" >}}) still shows the older day prices for buses and lorries (700 and 1,700 dinars).

## Who does not pay

People whose permanent address (*prebivalište*) is in one of these 20 municipalities do not pay for their car: Irig, Beočin, Sremski Karlovci, Novi Sad, Inđija, Ruma, Sremska Mitrovica, Šid, Bačka Palanka, Bač, Bački Petrovac, Bogatić, Odžaci, Stara Pazova, Šabac, Temerin, Titel, Vrbas, Zrenjanin, Žabalj. That includes Rakovac (Beočin). No registration is needed: their cars are recorded only for statistics ([official list](https://enaplatafruskagora.co.rs/Home/MunicipalityExemptionInfo), [021.rs](https://www.021.rs/novi-sad/vesti/460375/pocinje-elektronsko-evidentiranje-i-naplata-za-koriscenje-motornih-vozila-na-fruskoj-gori-ovo-su-detalji)). Some media write "21 municipalities" because Ruma appears twice in the table.

By [law](https://www.parlament.gov.rs/upload/archive/files/cir/pdf/zakoni/14_saziv/153-26.pdf) (article 109 as amended in 2026) people with disabilities, pre-school children, pensioners with the lowest pension, people working in the park and others are exempt too. If the system does not recognise you, for example because your car has plates from another region or another country, use the [exemption form](https://enaplatafruskagora.co.rs/Home/ExemptionRequest) with a scan of your ID card and of the vehicle registration (*saobraćajna dozvola*), and follow it on the [status page](https://enaplatafruskagora.co.rs/Home/ExemptionRequestStatus). Companies pay: owners of company vehicles receive a notice with a deadline.

How the exemption works for foreigners with temporary residence (*privremeni boravak*) whose car has local plates has not been explained officially: the system tells cars apart by their plates, while the law speaks of *prebivalište*. If you receive a charge, apply for the exemption or ask the park.

## Driving through, for example Rakovac – Ruma

For a driver who is exempt, as anyone registered in Rakovac or Beočin, it costs nothing. Everyone else pays the one-hour pass, 100 dinars for a car, or a day ticket, 300.

## How to pay

- **Online:** enter your plate (no spaces or dashes) on [enaplatafruskagora.co.rs](https://enaplatafruskagora.co.rs/). "Provera zaduženja" shows recorded passes with time, place, photo and amount; "Plati unapred" pays before the trip. By card or with a payment slip at a bank, the post office or by mobile banking ([user guide](https://enaplatafruskagora.co.rs/Content/documents/fruskagora/Korisnicko-uputstvo.pdf)). The portal is also linked from the [park's website](https://npfruskagora.co.rs/).
- **In person:** the [Information Centre on Iriški Venac]({{< relref "nearby/fruska-gora-information-centre" >}}) is closed for reconstruction. Advice in person at the park management in Sremska Kamenica (see Contacts).
